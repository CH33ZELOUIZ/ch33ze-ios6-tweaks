NavTunes

Navidrome downloads inside the iOS 6 Music app

What is new in 0.3.0

  • Progress rows now show playlist and album download totals, live percent, and speed.
  • Downloads can be retried, imported, played, or cancelled from the Downloads screen.
  • Settings includes connection testing, auto-import toggle, cache cleanup, queue count, and bug-report diagnostics.
  • Playlist handling avoids direct stock Music database edits by default to reduce launch crashes and library corruption risk.

Setup

Open Music, choose the Navidrome tab, then Settings. Enter your Navidrome/Subsonic server, username, and password or token/salt.

Preference file: /var/mobile/Library/Preferences/com.ch33ze.navtunes.plist

Known iOS 6 limitations

Imported tracks may take a moment to appear in the stock Music library. Keep Downloads open to see the exact state. Direct playlist DB updates remain off unless advanced testing explicitly enables them.
